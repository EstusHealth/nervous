The four pillars

What Hormozi measures, and what we swap

Pillar 1

Availability

Have open slots soon. Book the first appointment within 72 hours.

For us: keep short-notice slots open in the lead calendar, so a welcome call can happen this week.

Pillar 2

Speed

First contact in under 5 minutes. Every hour of delay costs bookings.

For us: the email goes out on its own. Your text follows the same business day, or first thing next morning.

Pillar 3

Personalisation

Their name, their goal, their preferred channel. Feels like a person, not a machine.

For us: read the referral before you text, and say so. Ask how they like to talk. Many neurodivergent adults dread surprise calls.

Pillar 4

Volume

Many touches. Gyms make up to 10 calls plus texts in week one.

For us: three emails and three texts in week one. Then we say we are stopping, and we stop.

The shape

Three phases

Minute 0 to day 1

Strike

Fast first contact, then a named person, on a channel they can answer in their own time.

Day 2 to day 7

Persist

Each touch gives something: the path, an easy question, a way out. Day 7 closes the loop politely.

Week 2 onward

Long nurture

One useful idea a month until they are ready. People come back months later. Stay useful, stay findable.

Health, not gyms

What we keep, what we drop

Gym Launch uses testimonials, scarcity and pressure. Registered health practitioners can't. Keep the speed and the persistence. Drop the pressure.

No testimonials

Gym"Here's what Sarah said after 6 weeks..."Us"Here's what happens on the call." Explain the process instead.

No promised outcomes

Gym"Lose 10 kg in 6 weeks."UsNever "we'll get you passed" or "fix your focus". Describe what we assess and how we help.

No fake urgency

Gym"Only 3 spots left, offer ends Friday."UsReal info only: "Next opening is Thursday." A real deadline the client told you about is fine to act on.

Consent and opt-out

GymCall until they answer.UsSay who you are in every message. Offer a clear "reply STOP" or "not now". Stop when asked.

Channel respect

GymDouble dial, every time.UsCall only if they chose phone. Text or email first is the default for neurodivergent adults.

No funding promises

Gym"It pays for itself."UsNever say or imply the NDIS will fund it. The line is "NDIS and private clients welcome".

Referrer privacy

Gymn/aUsUpdate the GP or support coordinator only with the client's consent. Thank them for the referral either way.
