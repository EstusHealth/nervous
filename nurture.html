Lead nurture, the Gym Launch way, rebuilt for OT

Hormozi's gyms won on one thing: they got back to people fast and kept going until the person said yes, no, or later. This trainer teaches that sequence, then shows it applied to ongoing online OT and in-person medical fitness to drive assessments.

5 min

Speed to lead

The core rule. Reply to a new enquiry inside five minutes. Odds of reaching and qualifying a lead fall off a cliff after that. The lead is warmest the moment they hit submit.

The four pillars

What Hormozi measures, and what we swap

PILLAR 1

Availability

Have open slots soon. Book the first appointment within 72 hours.

For us: clinicians keep short-notice slots in the lead calendar so welcome calls happen fast.

PILLAR 2

Speed

First contact in under 5 minutes. Every hour of delay costs bookings.

For us: instant auto-text after hours, a human reply first thing next morning.

PILLAR 3

Personalisation

Their name, their goal, their preferred channel. Feels like a person, not a machine.

For us: ask "call, text or email?" on the form. Many neurodivergent adults hate surprise calls. Honour it.

PILLAR 4

Volume

Many touches. Gyms make up to 10 calls plus texts in week one.

For us: fewer, clearer touches. Tell them how many you'll send. Always an easy "not now".

The shape

Three phases

MIN 0 TO DAY 1

Strike

Fast first contact, a second attempt with a new angle, then switch channel.

DAY 2 TO DAY 7

Persist

Daily touches. Each one gives something: help, info, a simple question. Day 7 closes the loop politely.

WEEK 2 ONWARD

Long nurture

Monthly value until they're ready. People come back months later. Stay useful, stay findable.

Then a separate job: once booked, get them to show up. A booking that no-shows is worth nothing. See tab 3.

Health, not gyms

What we keep, what we drop

Gym Launch uses testimonials, scarcity and pressure. Registered health practitioners can't. Keep the speed and the persistence. Drop the pressure.

No testimonials

Gym"Here's what Sarah said after 6 weeks..."Us"Here's what a first session looks like." Explain the process instead.

No promised outcomes

Gym"Lose 10 kg in 6 weeks."UsNever "we'll get you passed" or "fix your focus". Describe what we assess and how we help.

No fake urgency

Gym"Only 3 spots left, offer ends Friday."UsReal info only: "Next opening is Thursday." Real deadlines are fine to mention (a DoT due date the client told you).

Consent and opt-out

GymCall until they answer.UsSay who you are in every message. Offer a clear "reply STOP" or "not now". Stop when asked.

Channel respect

GymDouble dial, every time.UsDouble dial only if they chose phone. Text-first is the default for neurodivergent adults.

Referrer privacy

Gymn/aUsUpdate the GP or support coordinator only with the client's consent. Thank them for the referral either way.
