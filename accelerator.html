The program

Three phases

Same idea as the client journey: each phase has a gate. You move on when it's cleared.

  1. FoundationMonths 0 to 8

    You build

    Neuroaffirming foundations across PDA, ADHD and autism. Systems so documentation never eats your evenings. How the practice runs: referrals, welcome calls, bookings, billing. Fortnightly Clinician Checks. A team around you from day one.

    Cleared when

    You're a confident, independent clinician.

  2. SpecialisationMonths 8 to 18

    You build

    Pick your tracks. Start mentoring newer staff and writing team procedures. Learn the numbers: rebooking, cancellations, billing. Run group programs, webinars and community sessions. Monthly masterclasses.

    Cleared when

    You're a specialist with leadership reps.

  3. LeadershipMonths 18 to 36

    You build

    Senior clinician or clinical lead with full caseload autonomy. Hands-on practice management: hiring, onboarding, service design. Your own referral pipeline and professional brand. Speaking and writing.

    Cleared when

    You can lead a team, or build your own practice.

Career architecture

Four leadership pathways

Not everyone wants to run a practice. Lead the way that fits you.

Path A · Depth

Clinical Lead

Deep clinical expertise. Lead complex caseloads, supervise junior clinicians, shape protocols, become the specialist others refer to.

Path B · People

Team Lead

Lead a clinical pod. Team performance, caseload coordination, mentoring new staff, owning the rhythm of the team.

Path C · Operations

Practice Manager

The business brain. Revenue modelling, service design, hiring, systems. Build the infrastructure great clinicians work in.

Path D · Content

Thought Leader

Write, speak and build an audience. Publish clinical insights, present, and build your professional brand.

Why four? Clinical and operational leadership are different jobs. A great clinician shouldn't have to become a manager to progress.
