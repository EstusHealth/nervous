Motivational interviewing

Before any technique

The spirit of MI

Techniques without the spirit feel like tricks. People can tell.

Partnership

We work with them, not on them. They're the expert on their own life.

Acceptance

Respect their choices, their pace, and their right to say no.

Compassion

Their wellbeing comes first. Not our targets, not our plans.

Their reasons

The motivation is already in them. We help draw it out, not put it in.

Scope. Estus OTs use these as informed approaches inside OT goals: daily routines, participation and doing what matters. We are not delivering psychological therapy. If someone needs treatment for a mental health condition, we refer to a psychologist and work alongside them.

Listen for

Change talk vs sustain talk

Change talk is the client arguing for change. The more they say it, the more likely it happens.

DDesireI want to get to school on time.
AAbilityI could probably do it with a checklist.
RReasonsThen I wouldn't get in trouble.
NNeedI have to sort this before year 11.
CCommitmentI'm going to try it this week.
AActivationI'm ready to give it a go.
TTaking stepsI set two alarms last night.
SSustain talkIt's fine the way it is.
When you hear sustain talk, don't argue. Reflect it, then look for the other side: "It's working OK for now. What would make you want to change it, if anything?"

The trap

The righting reflex

The urge to fix it for them. It comes from caring, and it backfires.

Do

  • Ask before advising"Would it be OK if I shared an idea?"
  • Roll with discordIf they push back, step back. Reflect, don't argue.
  • Let them say the solutionPeople believe what they hear themselves say.
  • Give processing timeOne question, then silence. Typing in chat is fine.

Don't

  • Argue for changeIf we take the "change" side, they take the other one.
  • Stack questionsThree questions in a row is an interview, not a conversation.
  • Use clever wordingLiteral questions beat tricky ones, especially for autistic clients.
  • Treat MI as a sales tacticIt's never about getting compliance.
