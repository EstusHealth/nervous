Two seats

Player or GM?

You're still the clinician. GM Mode is a seat you can step into whenever the week starts running you.

Player seat

  • Reacts to every hit as it lands
  • Every worry feels like an order
  • Says yes to every request
  • The calendar fills from the outside in
  • Habits run on motivation

GM seat

  • Sees the whole season, not just this play
  • Worries are reports to read
  • Decides from the mission
  • The calendar is built from values first
  • Habits run on the schedule
This is ACT. Stepping up to the box is self-as-context: you're the one noticing, not the noise. The rest of the page is the other five skills, given a job in the front office.

Values on the calendar

The schedule is the strategy

The master calendar already puts anchors in first. GM Mode adds the why: every protected block is a value you can see. If it's not on the master calendar, it doesn't exist.

Book it like a clientSame respect, same notice to move it. You wouldn't cancel a client for an email.
Move it, don't delete itIf a block gets bumped, rebook it the same week.
Rest is a value blockRecovery protects every other value. Book it first.

Pick up to three values. Give each one a protected block. It opens with an example week.





Protected doesn't mean rigid. For ADHD or PDA profiles, loose blocks and choices often hold better than strict times. Same rules we use with clients on the Calendar page.





Committed action
Habits are reps, not moods
Committed action means doing the small thing again and again. A habit is what that looks like after enough reps. Four rules.


TinyTwo minutes to start. Small enough to do on a bad day.
AnchoredAfter something that already happens. Your transition prompt is the cue.
BookedOn the master calendar, with a time. Not "sometime".
ReviewedChecked every week in film review. Adjust, don't judge.


Never miss twice. Missing once is normal. The rep after a miss is the one that counts.
