The idea

Four beats, every time

"Must be nice" is the thought people have when they watch someone find the thing easy. We say it out loud, then show it was never about ease. Tap a beat.

Inversion in one lineDon't ask how to make it easy. Ask what would guarantee it stays hard. Then take one of those things away. That one thing is the small hinge.
It's an inside jokePeople share it because it's about them, not because it's an ad.
Kind by second threeThe sarcasm is the hook. The turn takes the sting out straight away.
Moment, not conditionThe line never says ADHD or autism. The right people know.
Inversion does the thinkingListing what keeps it hard is easy and a bit funny. The fix falls out.

Rules

Keep it kind, keep it a series

The format only works if the person living the moment laughs, not winces.

  • Fit, not easeNever us against them. Nobody has it easy. Their setup fits their brain.
  • Sarcasm ends by second threeDeadpan the line, then turn straight away.
  • Moment, not conditionNo diagnosis in the line or on screen. Name what happens.
  • Joke about the setupNever about the person, their family or their effort.
  • One hinge onlyOne small thing to try. Not five tips.
  • Try, never promise"One thing to try" is fine. "This will fix it" is not.
  • Same first frameThe line, big and centred, every time. That's how a series gets recognised.
  • Number them"Must be nice #7" in the caption. People go looking for the rest.

Do

  • Use staff or actors. Hands, backs and props work fine.
  • Use the oddly specific detail. "Since 2009" beats "for ages".
  • End on the hinge and one plain last line.
  • Post these as normal posts first.

Don't

  • No client stories, names, faces or plan details.
  • No "do you have ADHD?" or "your autism".
  • No testimonials, reviews or results.
  • No "normal people". There's no them.

Before you put money behind one as an ad, run it through the Reels copy check. That check flags the word "guarantee". In the anti-recipe it's about the bad outcome, which is the joke. Never use it about a result.
