The formula

The 6-beat script

About 30 seconds, 70 to 100 spoken words. Tap a beat.

  • Captions burned in, centred, 2 to 4 words at a time. Most people watch on mute.
  • A cut or new angle every 3 to 6 seconds.
  • End on a line that loops back into the hook.
  • Add the funding line in beat 5 or 6: "NDIS and private clients welcome".

Hook bank

Name a moment, not a condition

Each hook is acted out in the first 2 seconds. Filter by audience, copy the one you want, or send it to the builder.

Small Hinges offer line for beat 6: "It's called Small Hinges. Pick one area, one session at a time." Test two CTAs: book a call vs the free Small Hinges webinar or guide.
