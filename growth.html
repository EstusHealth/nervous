07 · House rules

Kind, honest, ours

Every post, ad, page and email. The copy check catches most of these. The rest is judgement.

  • The momentName a moment everyone recognises. Never a condition, and never "do you have ADHD?".
  • The hookOne complete sentence, one block, one size, one colour. Any "parents often say" lead-in sits inside that sentence.
  • ToneWarm, plain and a bit funny. Laugh with, never at. No "fix", "normal" or "suffers".
  • ClaimsSay what we work on and how. No promised outcomes or timeframes, no testimonials or reviews.
  • Funding"NDIS and private clients welcome". Never imply the NDIS will pay.
  • UrgencyReal availability only. No countdowns or made-up spot counts.
  • PeopleStaff who have said yes, or actors. Never clients, their homes or their details.
  • Pictures and soundOur footage and pictures Claude draws. Music from the app's own library.
  • DataNo health details in URLs, forms sent to Meta, or pixel events. No client lists uploaded.
  • WritingPlain words, short lines, Australian spelling, one CTA. No em-dashes.
