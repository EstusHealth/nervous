An ACT game · UFC 94 · Las Vegas · 31 January 2009

Rematch With Fear

Georges St-Pierre (GSP) was one of the greatest fighters ever. He also said he was scared before every single fight. He fought anyway. In this game you walk his path, from the locker room to the cage and back to the gym, and learn the six ACT skills on the way.

ACT in one line

You don't have to get rid of fear to do what matters. You can bring it with you.

ACT is Acceptance and Commitment Therapy. This game teaches the ideas. It isn't therapy. If fear or worry is getting in the way of life, talk to a health professional.
