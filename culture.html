The why

What we believe

If you remember nothing else, remember these.

Your ceiling is high

Early-career clinicians are underestimated across allied health. We respect what it took to get here, and how far you can go.

Extreme ownership

Own what's in your control. Influence what isn't. Don't waste energy reacting to what you can do nothing about.

Burnout is a mismatch

Burnout comes from effort and reward not lining up, not only from long hours. We fix the mismatch.

Systems over willpower

Change the environment to match the work. If something relies on willpower at 6pm, the system is wrong.

Niche on purpose

We are very clear about who we see. We say no to referrals outside our niche, so every client is the right fit.

Leaders build infrastructure

A leader's job is not to do your job. It's to give you the resources, training and systems to do it brilliantly.

The operating model

How we work

A different model to most allied health employers. On purpose.

WhatHow it works at Estus
WhereTelehealth, Australia-wide. A Perth clinic opens in 2027. No driving between home visits.
EmploymentMostly casual, with a strong hourly rate plus super. The more you work, the more you earn. Insurance is covered.
Portfolio incomeYou can add contracting on the side, like functional assessment interviews with a partner practice. If you want to start your own business, we help with that too.
Your calendarYou set your availability. Clients book themselves into it through online booking, and manage their own changes.
NotesSessions are transcribed in Google Workspace. Notes are a single click at the end of the session.
Between sessionsBuilt-in breaks between telehealth sessions. Eat, walk, move. Admin between sessions means a system needs fixing.
MeasuresA few clear thresholds in the Clinician Check, not billable hours for their own sake.
The aim: you can see exactly how your income is built and what moves it. Your career should feel like it's in your hands, because it is.

How we lead

Leadership at Estus

Leadership is a skill. We train it like any other.

Two kinds of leadership

Clinical leadership and operational leadership are different jobs. A great clinician doesn't have to become a manager to progress.

High warmth, high standards

Supportive and demanding at once. No micromanaging, no ruling by fear, no people-pleasing.

Three questions

What's going well? What isn't? What could be improved? Asked often, in a safe space, about us too.

Overcommunicate the why

People forget the purpose when it's said once. We repeat it, in more than one format.

Effort counts twice

Angela Duckworth: talent times effort becomes skill, and skill times effort becomes achievement. True for leading as well as treating.

Ownership in community

On our Discord and Minecraft server, young people get real roles and responsibilities. We model the same ownership we ask of them.
