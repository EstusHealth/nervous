      Search one word, or filter by B-roll or podcast clips and a theme. Gaming clips are our best organic content, so start there.

      Every hook is one complete sentence, shown as one block in one style. Copy it as is, or rewrite it and keep it one sentence.

      Tap Open to go straight to the file in Drive. Edited shorts live in Media > 04 Shorts > Ready to post, one folder per short. Raw cuts live in Media > 02 Podcast clips.

      Tap Copy edit prompt, then attach the file in Claude. Or take it to Shorts Studio, pick Podcast or webinar clip and Claude edits my clip.

      Tick Posted on the card. Ticks save in your browser only.
